Draw&Bracket Team maker · knockout scheduler · in-browser
Two ways to bracket a tournament

Paste names, generate randomized teams, and click through a bracket to advance winners. Everything runs in your browser — nothing gets uploaded, nothing signs you up. Pick the format that fits your group and go.

Mode 01 · Single-elimination

Classic Knockout

Straight-up single-elimination. Best when your count is close to a power of two, or when you just want the cleanest bracket possible.

  • Rolling byes — one at a time, only when a round has an odd count
  • Bye recipient picked at random each round (no top-seed advantage)
  • 11 teams → 2 byes total, not 5

Mode 02 · Knockout + round-robin

Hybrid Bracket

Round 1 is knockout. From round 2 onward, any odd round becomes a round-robin group with the top 2 advancing. Avoids compounding byes deeper in the bracket.

  • Everyone plays multiple games when a group forms
  • Standings by W–L record, seed as tiebreaker
  • Better for small-to-mid brackets with awkward team counts

Mode 03 · Excel import · best-of-3

Excel Import

Already drew the bracket by hand? Paste the table from Excel and get a clickable best-of-3 bracket. Auto-appends later rounds as classic knockout.

  • Handles multi-section paste — Initial Round, Quarter Final, Semi Final, Final
  • Resolves ? placeholders and numeric "winner of match N" refs
  • Click G1 / G2 / G3 per side — first to 2 games wins the series

Paste or upload

Names go in the textarea, or bring a .txt / .csv file. Team size is configurable up to 12.

Additive draw

Add names later — existing teams stay put, new names fill partial teams first, then form new teams.

Persistent state

Teams, bracket, and every winner pick save to localStorage. Survives reloads until you hit Clear.

Export & Import

Download JSON as backup (round-trips full state), or a printable text summary. Import restores after accidents.

Light & dark

Follows your OS by default. Toggle in either page's masthead any time.

No server

All logic is local. No accounts, no analytics, no requests off your machine.
