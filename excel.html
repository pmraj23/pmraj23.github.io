01 Paste from Excel

TSV format — select cells in Excel, Cmd+C, paste here

Format

  1. Select the whole table in Excel (including header rows) and copy.
  2. Paste here. Columns should be tab-separated (Excel does this automatically).
  3. Each round starts with a header row where column 2 is Initial Round, Quarter Final, Semi Final, Final, or Round N.
  4. Match rows: Match#, Team A, vs, Team B, Note.
  5. Use ? for a TBD slot, or a number like 4 meaning "winner of match 4 in the previous round".

Best-of-3

Each match is a series of up to 3 games. Click the game boxes (G1 / G2 / G3) on the winning side of a match to record games. Whoever wins 2 games first advances.

Rounds after your paste

Later rounds you didn't include are auto-appended as classic knockout — one match per pair — until a single Final remains.
